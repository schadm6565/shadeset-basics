Pricing basis

What the numbers mean.

Snapshot prices

Creative Glamour prices in South African rand, VAT included, recorded on 17 October 2026. Local Pretoria/Johannesburg delivery estimated at R95. Not live stock and not a checkout.

Fair comparison

For each look we find every cheapest kit, then pick the combination most favourable to buying separately. Identical configurations count once, and both sides pay one delivery.

Approximate colour

Previews come from the YouCam Makeup VTO API on a synthetic face. Screen colour doesn't guarantee how physical makeup looks. No retailer partnership.
