A practice to reuse

The requirement “compare fairly” was made concrete before implementation: keep every equally cheap filler configuration, then minimise the shared purchase. test/optimiser.test.js checks all nine cheapest custom-four choices for each sample recipe and confirms R550 versus R743. An independent exhaustive check guards against an algorithm that merely matches the expected sample numbers.

Define the observable behaviour first. A passing sample alone would not prove that equally cheap alternatives were handled fairly.

To change this, start here

  • Recipes, placements and catalogue offers: src/catalogue.js.
  • Exact product source URLs and component prices: src/sources.js.
  • Basket mathematics: src/optimiser.js, with behaviour checks in test/optimiser.test.js.
  • Layout and typography: src/style.css and index.html.
  • Approval identity component: src/approval.js, with photo/recipe/output invalidation checks in test/approval.test.js. Connected through src/previews.js; genuine assets remain unavailable.
